# Regional landforms, version 5

New campaigns select among 14 broad landforms. Local provinces blend the chosen form with climate-weighted alternatives. Terrain height is evaluated at fixed seed/coordinates before sea level, climate selection, drainage and resource placement. No pass, river or mountain overlay is painted onto a finished resource map.

## New patterns

| Pattern | Physical shape | Gameplay tendency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Drowned valleys | Branching incisions through a low coastal platform | Indented bays, inland sea access, multiple landing fronts |
| Volcanic arcs | Unequal massifs along curved chains, occasional collapsed centers | Steep island interiors, short rivers, naval links between islands |
| Basin and range | Parallel narrow uplifts with broader intervening valleys | Long inland corridors, restricted cross-range routes |
| Dissected plateaus | Broad raised surfaces cut by lower corridors | Upland settlement areas, exposed margins, valley crossings |

Dry interiors favor basin/range and plateau patterns. Humid non-polar provinces favor drowned valleys. Volcanic arcs have no forced climate: elevation and maritime exposure determine their climate through the same physical pipeline. Regional blending prevents hard seams. Formations repeat beyond the starting area with coordinate-based variation.

Lakes retain their 12-hex cap. The existing ocean-area reduction remains. Volcanic island catchments use short river courses; mainland patterns can carry longer converging courses. No eruptions, lava damage, new resource cards or permanent mountain crossings are added.

## Save compatibility

Version 5 is assigned only to new worlds. Versions 1–4 retain their original height fields, landform choices, sea levels and drainage. Explicit version parameters reach every physical-landform query. Regression fixtures record version 4 elevations, drainage and climate settings. Expedition additions leave revealed terrain intact.

## Geological references

The shapes are board-game approximations, not geological simulations. The formulas and gameplay weights are design choices.

- [USGS landforms map](https://www.usgs.gov/educational-resources/landforms-map-conterminous-united-states): drowned river-valley coasts and continental relief.
- [National Park Service: Basin and Range](https://www.nps.gov/articles/basinrange.htm): alternating ranges and basins.
- [National Park Service: Colorado Plateau and Basin and Range](https://www.nps.gov/para/learn/nature/stretching-of-the-basin-and-range-and-lifting-of-the-colorado-plateau.htm): uplifted plateaus and fault-block terrain.
- [USGS: Volcanoes](https://pubs.usgs.gov/gip/volc/text.html): chains of volcanic islands.
- [USGS: Crater Lake](https://www.usgs.gov/volcanoes/crater-lake/science/post-caldera-volcanism-and-crater-lake): collapsed volcanic centers and later landforms.
